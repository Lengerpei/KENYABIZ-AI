# KenyaBiz AI - Company Profile

KenyaBiz Supplies is a fictional Kenyan startup created
for the KenyaBiz AI capstone project.

The company supplies office furniture, office equipment,
and technology accessories to businesses and individuals
across Kenya.

## Main Products

- Office chairs
- Office desks
- Executive desks
- Visitor chairs
- Office cabinets
- Meeting tables
- Laptop stands
- Monitor stands
- Keyboards
- Wireless mice

## Services

KenyaBiz Supplies provides:

- Product information
- Price quotations
- Order processing
- Invoice generation
- Payment assistance
- Delivery coordination

## Business Location

The company operates in Kenya and serves customers
within Nairobi and other parts of the country.

## Currency

All product prices are expressed in Kenyan Shillings (KES).

## Ordering

Customers can request a quotation before confirming
an order.

An invoice is generated after an order is confirmed.

Payment status remains pending until payment is confirmed.

## Important

This is a demonstration business created for the
Mastering AI Agents capstone project.
