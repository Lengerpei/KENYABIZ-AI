# Delivery Policy

## Nairobi

Typical delivery time is 2-3 business days.

## Outside Nairobi

Typical delivery time is 3-7 business days.

## Delivery Charges

Delivery charges depend on the destination,
order size, and delivery requirements.

For the capstone demonstration, a configurable
delivery charge will be used.

## Delivery Status

Possible delivery statuses:

- PENDING
- PROCESSING
- DISPATCHED
- DELIVERED

An order remains pending until payment confirmation
has been received.
