# Frequently Asked Questions

## What products do you sell?

We sell office furniture, office equipment,
and technology accessories.

## Can I request a quotation?

Yes. Customers can request quotations based on
the products and quantities they require.

## Can I order multiple products?

Yes. A customer can include multiple products
in a single quotation or order.

## Do you deliver?

Yes. KenyaBiz Supplies provides delivery services
within Nairobi and other parts of Kenya.

## When is an invoice generated?

An invoice is generated after the customer confirms
the quotation and the order is created.

## How can I pay?

The system supports payment assistance through
a simulated M-PESA payment workflow for this
capstone demonstration.

## Can I ask questions before buying?

Yes. The customer support agent can answer questions
about products, company policies, delivery,
payments, and ordering.
