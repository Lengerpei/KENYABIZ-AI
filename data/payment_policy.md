# Payment Policy

Customers receive an invoice after confirming an order.

The capstone application uses a simulated payment
request rather than a live payment gateway.

Payment statuses include:

- PENDING
- PAYMENT_REQUESTED
- PAID
- FAILED

The payment simulator generates a payment reference
for demonstration purposes.

Example:

Payment Reference: KB-PAY-00001
Amount: KES 75,000
Status: PENDING
